Day 1 · Mar 24

1. The Night Before — Newark

It started in Newark, New Jersey. The Icelandair plane waiting at the gate in the evening dark, Icelandic krona counted out and packed, and later, somewhere over the Atlantic, the in-flight magazine already promising glaciers.

Day 2 · Mar 25

2. Touchdown at 3:55 AM

Keflavík at 03:55 — Iceland in full winter dark. The Voyage statues greeted us outside the terminal, and a small white VW Polo from Lotus Car Rental (38,500 ISK, winter tires) became our home for five days. The mistake: the car. In March you want an SUV, all-wheel drive, and chains — cars get stuck. And take the top-tier insurance: when cars get stuck in the weather, the company comes and picks YOU up and leaves the car behind. We passed plenty of abandoned cars on the roadside.

Day 2 · Mar 25

3. The Golden Circle: Þingvellir, Gullfoss & Geysir

By mid-morning the plateau was doing its thing: snow flurries over the Þingvellir rift — where you can literally walk from the North American plate to the Eurasian plate — then steam hissing off Geysir's basin, and Gullfoss roaring at ~200 m elevation, a wide horseshoe of water freezing mid-air with tiny figures in red and yellow jackets standing on the ice below for scale.

Day 2 · Mar 25

4. Evening in Reykjavík — HI Downtown

Back over the mountains to the capital. Check-in at HI Downtown, Vesturgata 17 — a warm room with a window over the street, and the courtyard's flag-painted cows. Then a walk through the wet streets: the BONUS sign glowing neon, Hallgrímskirkja's stained glass, Skólastræti at 21:56.

Day 3 · Mar 26

5. Seljalandsfoss — Behind the Waterfall

Morning roads south along the coast, snow on the verges. Then the first big stop: Seljalandsfoss, where a path leads right behind the falling water — standing in the cave between the cliff and the curtain of spray.

Day 3 · Mar 26

6. Skógafoss & the Little White Polo

Twenty minutes further east, Skógafoss — 60 metres of water off a flat cliff top. This is where the rental car finally got its portrait: the little white Polo behind us on the viewing platform, its mirror reflecting the falls. Two-wheel drive and all — it got us this far.

Day 3 · Mar 26

7. The Ring Road — the DC-3 Wreck

The ring road unspooled under a grey sky, and the famous DC-3 plane wreck sat on the black sands of Sólheimasandur, half a century after the US Navy plane crash-landed there. Snowy mountain passes with sun flare on the way down; by 20:28 the headlights were on Suðurvíkurvegur in Vík.

Days 3-4 · Mar 26-28

8. Two Nights in Vík

Hostel Vík, Suðurvíkurvegur 5 — Room 9 upstairs, key in the pen holder. Reynisfjara's black sand and basalt columns with waves crashing, the red-roofed church on its hill, Icelandic horses in the snow fields, rice cooking in the hostel kitchen. Two full nights on the south coast.

Day 4 · Mar 27

9. Jökulsárlón & Diamond Beach

Two and a half hours east to the glacier lagoon: icebergs calved from Vatnajökull drift in the grey water, then wash up on the black sand of Diamond Beach — translucent blue jewels scattered to the horizon, waves crashing over them.

Day 4 · Mar 27

10. Inside the Glacier

Ice Explorers' Super Jeep bounced across the glacier tongue to the cave mouth. Inside, the Vatnajökull ice glowed electric blue — light streaming through the ceiling, helmets and headlamps against textured walls. 194 photos and 174 clips later, it was still the best hour of the trip.

Day 5 · Mar 28

11. Blue Lagoon

West again — a drive-by of Ölfus at 21 km/h (photos from a moving car), then Grindavík. 130 photos of the milky-blue water: 39°C steam curling off the lava field into the March cold, snow on the banks, silhouettes in the warmth.

Days 5-6 · Mar 28-29

12. Last Night in Reykjavík

HI City, Sundlaugavegur 34. Lækjargata at 23:00, the harbor the next morning, one more walk through the city — then FI623 home. 739 km on the little Polo, 1,257 files in the archive, and a story that started at 3:55 AM.
