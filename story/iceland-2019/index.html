1. Touchdown at 3:55 AM

The flight landed at Keflavík at 03:55 — Iceland in full winter dark. A small white VW Polo from Lotus Car Rental, 38,500 ISK with winter tires, and we rolled out onto the Reykjanes peninsula. The first photos are jet-lagged and dark at Garður; the adventure clock starts here.

Day 2 · Mar 25

2. The Golden Circle

By mid-morning the plateau was doing its thing: snow flurries over the Þingvellir rift, steam hissing off Geysir's basin, Gullfoss roaring at ~200 m elevation. Cold hands, phone cameras out — 26 photos at the first stop alone, the land steaming and frozen at once.

Day 2 · Mar 25

3. Evening in Reykjavík — HI Downtown

Back over the mountains to the capital. Check-in at HI Downtown, Vesturgata 17: a warm room with a window over the street, the courtyard's flag-painted cows, then a walk downtown — Skólastræti at 21:56, wet streets and wooden houses.

Day 3 · Mar 26

4. A Capital Day, Then South

A full day around Kópavogur and the capital's west — 289 photos, the biggest day of the trip. Then the Polo turned south on Suðurlandsvegur, the ring road unspooling under a grey sky, and by 20:28 the headlights were on Suðurvíkurvegur in Vík.

Days 3-4 · Mar 26-28

5. Two Nights in Vík

Hostel Vík, Suðurvíkurvegur 5 — Room 9 upstairs, key in the pen holder. The red-roofed church on its hill, Reynisfjara's black sand and basalt columns, snow on the paths, rice cooking in the hostel kitchen. The south coast's dark beauty for two full nights.

Day 4 · Mar 27

6. The Ice Cave — Jökulsárlón

Out the door at 07:04, two and a half hours east to the glacier lagoon. Ice Explorers' Super Jeep, the Vatnajökull ice cave glowing electric blue — 194 photos and 174 clips later, it was still the best hour of the trip.

Day 5 · Mar 28

7. Blue Lagoon

West again — a drive-by of Ölfus at 21 km/h (photos from a moving car), then Grindavík. 130 photos of the milky-blue water, 39°C steam curling off the lava field in the March cold.

Days 5-6 · Mar 28-29

8. Last Night in Reykjavík

HI City, Sundlaugavegur 34. Lækjargata at 23:00 on the last night, Hallgrímskirkja tower in the morning light, a final city day — then FI623 home. 739 km on the little Polo.
