Day 1 · Mar 24

1. The Night Before — Newark

Newark, the night before. Icelandair at the gate in the dark, krona counted out and packed, and somewhere over the Atlantic the in-flight magazine already had us hyped for glaciers.

Day 2 · Mar 25

2. Touchdown at 3:55 AM

Landed Keflavík at 3:55 AM in full winter dark. The Voyage statues outside the terminal, then the little white VW Polo from Lotus Car Rental — 38,500 ISK with winter tires. Mistake: it should've been an SUV with all-wheel drive and chains; cars get stuck out here. And get the top-tier insurance — when you get stuck, they come pick YOU up and leave the car behind. We passed plenty of abandoned cars on the roadside.

Day 2 · Mar 25

3. The Golden Circle: Þingvellir, Gullfoss & Geysir

The Golden Circle in one morning. Þingvellir, where you walk from the North American plate to the Eurasian plate (we got video of that). Then Geysir's steam — the name comes from here — and Gullfoss, a huge two-tier waterfall half frozen, with people in red and yellow jackets standing on the ice below looking tiny.

Day 2 · Mar 25

4. Evening in Reykjavík — HI Downtown

Back to the capital. Check-in at HI Downtown, Vesturgata 17 — warm room with a window over the street, flag-painted cows in the courtyard. Evening walk: BONUS glowing neon, Hallgrímskirkja's stained glass, Skólastræti at 21:56.

Day 3 · Mar 26

5. Seljalandsfoss — Behind the Waterfall

Morning roads south along the coast, snow on the verges. Seljalandsfoss: a path goes right behind the waterfall — you stand in a cave between the cliff and the curtain of spray. You get wet. It's worth it.

Day 3 · Mar 26

6. Skógafoss & the Little White Polo

Twenty minutes further east, Skógafoss — 60 metres of water off a flat cliff top. This is where the rental car finally got its photos: the little white Polo behind us on the viewing platform, its mirror reflecting the falls. Two-wheel drive and all, it got us this far — 739 km — and one last portrait with it at the Lotus lot before we handed the keys back.

Day 3 · Mar 26

7. The Ring Road — the DC-3 Wreck

The DC-3 wreck at Sólheimasandur: a US Navy plane that crash-landed here in 1973 — everyone walked away. It just sits on the black sand like a monument. Then snowy mountain passes with sun flare, and by 20:28 we were in Vík.

Days 3-4 · Mar 26-28

8. Two Nights in Vík

Hostel Vík, Suðurvíkurvegur 5 — Room 9 upstairs, key in the pen holder. Reynisfjara's black sand and basalt columns with waves crashing, the red-roofed church on its hill, Icelandic horses in the snow fields, rice cooking in the hostel kitchen. Two full nights on the south coast.

Day 4 · Mar 27

9. Jökulsárlón & Diamond Beach

Jökulsárlón: icebergs from Vatnajökull drifting in the grey water. Diamond Beach is right next door — the same icebergs wash up on black sand, like blue jewels scattered around, with waves crashing over them.

Day 4 · Mar 27

10. Inside the Glacier

Ice Explorers tour: a Super Jeep across the glacier tongue to the cave mouth. Inside, the Vatnajökull ice glows electric blue — light through the ceiling, helmets on. 194 photos and 174 clips later, it was still the best hour of the trip.

Day 5 · Mar 28

11. Blue Lagoon

West again — a drive-by of Ölfus where the tracker clocked us at 21 km/h (we were shooting from the moving car), then Grindavík. Blue Lagoon: milky blue water at 39°C, steam into the cold air, snow on the banks. Postcard stuff.

Days 5-6 · Mar 28-29

12. Last Night in Reykjavík

HI City, Sundlaugavegur 34. Lækjargata at 23:00, the harbor the next morning, one more walk through the city — then FI623 home. 739 km on the little Polo, 1,257 files, and a story that started at 3:55 AM.

The places

Every Place We Stopped

All of it, in plain words.

Keflavík Airport Day 2

Where we landed at 3:55 AM after the overnight flight. First thing you see outside is the Voyage statues. The Lotus Car Rental desk is right there — that's where the little white Polo came from.

Garður Day 2

Tiny fishing town on the Reykjanes peninsula, basically the airport's neighbor. Our first photos in the dark. Nothing else to report — it exists, we were jet-lagged.

Þingvellir Day 2

The rift valley where the tectonic plates meet. You can stand with one foot on the North American plate and one on the Eurasian. It's cold and windy and you can see the crack in the earth. There's video of the walk.

Geysir Day 2

The original geyser — the word comes from here. It mostly sleeps now, but Strokkur next to it erupts every few minutes. Steam everywhere.

Gullfoss Day 2

The big two-tier waterfall, half frozen in March. You can get right up to the edge and the spray soaks you. People in red and yellow jackets on the ice below look tiny.

Reykjavík (downtown) Day 2 + 5-6

Small-town capital. We stayed at HI Downtown (Vesturgata 17) on the first night and HI City (Sundlaugavegur 34) on the last. Walkable center: the BONUS grocery with the pink pig, Hallgrímskirkja, Laugavegur. Lækjargata at 23:00 on the last night.

Mosfellsbær Day 2

Suburb north of Reykjavík we drove through on Þingvallavegur coming back from the Golden Circle. Didn't stop — the tracker just noticed us passing.

Kópavogur Day 3

Reykjavík's biggest neighbor. Morning drive-around before pointing the car south. The tracker's anchor thought we lived there.

Seljalandsfoss Day 3

The waterfall you can walk behind. A path goes into a cave between the cliff and the water curtain. You get wet, it's worth it. Video included.

Skógafoss Day 3

Sixty metres of water off a flat cliff top, very wide. You can walk right up to it. Also where the rental car got its portrait — the little white Polo that should've been an SUV.

Sólheimasandur Day 3

Black sand beach with a crashed US Navy DC-3 from 1973. Everyone on board walked away. It just sits there on the sand. There's a long walk in from the road, or you can see it from a distance. Video included.

Vík í Mýrdal Days 3-4

The main village on the south coast. Red-roofed church on the hill, Hostel Vík on Suðurvíkurvegur, Room 9 upstairs with the key in the pen holder. Two nights here.

Reynisfjara Day 4

The black sand beach with basalt columns and big waves. Genuinely beautiful. Don't turn your back on the waves — the sneaker waves here have killed people.

Jökulsárlón Day 4

The glacier lagoon. Icebergs calved off Vatnajökull drift in the water. There's a café where the ice cave tours meet.

Diamond Beach Day 4

The black beach right next to the lagoon where the icebergs wash up. They look like diamonds scattered on black sand. Waves crash over them.

Vatnajökull ice cave Day 4

Ice Explorers take you in a Super Jeep across the glacier tongue to a natural cave. Inside, the ice glows blue and light comes through the ceiling. Best hour of the trip, and the biggest photo haul: 194 photos, 174 clips.

Ölfus / Selfoss Day 5

Drove through it on the way west. The tracker clocked us at 21 km/h here because we were taking photos from the moving car.

Grindavík Day 5

Fishing town next to Blue Lagoon. We only really saw it from the car, and the lagoon itself.

Blue Lagoon Day 5

The famous geothermal spa. Milky blue water at 39°C, steam rising into the cold, snow on the banks. Tourist-y and pricey but it's the postcard experience for a reason.

Keflavík (Reykjanesbær) Day 6

Back near the airport the afternoon before the flight. Killed time around the town before FI623.
